Simple from start to pickup

How SharePlate works

A simple handoff connects food businesses with nearby people and community groups. Every step is visible in the offer and account activity.

01 · List

Hotels and restaurants share surplus meals

Create a hotel or restaurant account, then list the food, number of meals, price per meal, neighborhood, pickup date and time window. Choose a club partner and cutoff time; unclaimed food switches to a free donation for that club.
02 · Reserve

People or clubs claim an offer

Browse and search current offers. Sign in as a community member or club and reserve a meal bundle. Each reservation reduces the remaining meal count. More than one group can claim an offer while meals are still available.
03 · Collect

Meet at the listed pickup time

The reservation appears under My activity with the hotel or restaurant, pickup location and time window. The receiver gets a one-time pickup code. The hotel or restaurant verifies the code at handoff.
Food Score

Recognize reliable contributions

Each food business starts at 50 points. It earns 10 points when it confirms a completed pickup. The score updates on its offers and account activity, making consistent food sharing easy to recognize.
